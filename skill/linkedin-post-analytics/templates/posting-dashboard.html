        About the data

        Post statistics are lifetime totals at collection, not activity during the publication date range. Unavailable values appear as —. Public engagements are reactions + comments + reposts; LinkedIn’s displayed social engagements additionally include saves and sends. All medians exclude missing data. Small samples and different post ages limit comparisons.
